# GEANOS Store IQ Tool-Family Colour Guide

Approved by GEANOS Giftware & Novelties.

This guide defines the official colour system for GEANOS Store IQ and its paid add-on tools. The colours help merchants recognise which family of tools they are using while preserving a professional Shopify experience.

## Official Colour System

| Tool family | Backing colour | Hex code |
|---|---|---|
| Main App | White | `#FFFFFF` |
| Photos & Videos | Light blue | `#EEF6FF` |
| Marketing | Light sage green | `#EFF8F1` |
| SEO | Soft greenish-yellow | `#F7F8E8` |
| Business Intelligence | Light lavender | `#F5F1FA` |
| Reporting | Soft blue-grey | `#F3F6F8` |

## Design Rules

- Use colours as subtle backing-sheet or card tints.
- Keep all body text dark and easy to read.
- Use a slightly stronger matching border or heading accent where helpful.
- Keep buttons clear and visually distinct from their backing sheet.
- Preserve Shopify’s standard warning, error, information and success colours.
- Use the same colour consistently throughout each tool family.
- Do not use strong, saturated or distracting versions of these colours.
- Maintain accessible contrast for text, buttons, links and controls.
- Treat the colours as lightly tinted paper rather than heavily painted panels.

## Tool-Family Guidance

### Main App

Use white for the main GEANOS Store IQ application, navigation hubs and general account areas.

### Photos & Videos

Use light blue for Photo Translator & Cleanup, Video Translator & Cleanup, media credit management and related media-processing areas.

### Marketing

Use light sage green for advertising, campaign planning, seasonal marketing and promotional tools.

### SEO

Use soft greenish-yellow for SEO analysis, content optimisation and search-visibility tools.

### Business Intelligence

Use light lavender for business analysis, recommendations, strategy and intelligence tools.

### Reporting

Use soft blue-grey for reports, exports, summaries, calendars and historical records.

## Implementation Order

1. Apply light blue to the Photo and Video Media Tools family.
2. Complete the paid Photo add-on.
3. Complete the paid Video add-on.
4. Apply the remaining colours as each tool family is expanded.
5. Review every colour in desktop and embedded Shopify views before release.
